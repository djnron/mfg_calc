# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community.

Participants should communicate constructively, assume good intent, respect differing experience levels, and focus criticism on ideas and implementation rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and abusive conduct are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Serious concerns may be reported privately through the security/contact process described in `SECURITY.md`.
